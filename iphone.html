Install Match Maker

on iPhone or iPad · JAMREIBIZ Badminton Club

Match Maker installs from this page in about a minute. It opens full screen like an app, works without internet and updates itself.

Scan with your iPhone camera

archie24bagadiong-max.github.io/matchmaker-app/iphone.html

  1. Tap Copy link for Safari above.
  2. Open Safari (the blue compass), tap the address bar, choose Paste and Go.
  3. Follow the steps on this page in Safari.

Option 1 · One-tap install recommended

Install Match Maker
  1. Tap Install Match Maker, then Allow.The iPhone says “Profile Downloaded”. Tap Close.
  2. Open Settings. At the top tap Profile Downloaded.Don’t see it? Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Match Maker.
  3. Tap Install (top right), enter your iPhone passcode, then Install → Install → Done.
  4. Go to the Home Screen and open Match Maker. 🎉
Why does it say “Not Verified”?

The club made this small install file itself, without paying Apple to sign it, so the iPhone shows Not Verified. It is safe: it only puts the Match Maker icon on your Home Screen. It cannot read, track or change anything on your phone.

To remove it later: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Match Maker → Remove Profile, or just press and hold the icon → Remove.

Option 2 · Add to Home Screen no settings needed

Open Match Maker in Safari
  1. Open Match Maker in Safari (button above).
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen.On iPad it is at the top right. On iOS 26, tap ⋯ first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Good to know

Your data stays on this iPhone: players, attendance, payments and history are saved in the app on the phone, even with no internet. Export a report now and then as a backup (Payments → Excel report).

Updates are automatic. Close the app and open it again to get the newest version (Settings shows the version).

Just scoring a court? No install needed: scan the court’s QR code on the organizer’s screen and score in the browser.
