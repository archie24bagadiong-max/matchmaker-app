iPhone

  1. Open the Camera and point it at the code.
  2. Tap the yellow link that appears.
  3. Optional: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the Camera (or Google Lens) and point it at the code.
  2. Tap the link that appears.
  3. Optional: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Nothing to download from a file — it opens in your browser and updates by itself.
